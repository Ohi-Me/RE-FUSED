# Deviations from the pre-registration of the Indian hourly study

Every change made after the pre-registration was frozen (tag `prereg-india-v1`, 6 October 2026 10:13 UTC) is listed
here with its reason and its possible effect on the results.

| # | Date | What changed | Why | Effect on results |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | 6 Oct 2026 | Thread limits: `make_learned_baselines_india.py` sets PyTorch to 4 compute threads and 1 inter-op thread and PyArrow to 4 threads; the job file sets 4 threads for OpenMP, MKL and OpenBLAS (was 8, none for PyTorch and PyArrow) | The cluster stopped the first two confirmatory attempts (H100 jobs 34212, 34213) within 35 s because the job briefly used more than the 8 cores it requested ("ncpus 9.0 exceeded limit 8 (burst)" in the PBS log); no forecast had been written | None on the method; the number of threads can change the last digits of floating-point sums on the CPU parts of training |
| D2 | 6 Oct 2026 | The job file pins the whole run to the 6 least-busy cores of the node (`taskset`), chosen from two seconds of `/proc/stat` at the start of the job | The third attempt (H100 job 34214) was stopped 17 s after it started, at the start of neural-network training, after using 9 min 19 s of CPU time (about 33 cores on average): one of the libraries ignores the thread settings of D1. Pinning makes the limit hold whatever the libraries do; no forecast had been written | None on the method or the data; as for D1, only the last digits of CPU floating-point sums can differ |
| D3 | 6 Oct 2026 | Number of attempts: §6 allows one rerun with the same code after a technical failure; the confirmatory run took four attempts (34212, 34213, 34214, 34223), with the thread and core changes of D1 and D2 in between | Each of the first three attempts was stopped by the cluster within 35 s, before any forecast, correction or score for the test months was produced (the panel had been loaded, and the 34214 attempt had fitted the first LightGBM model, which predicts May–Aug 2025 only; log `12_logs/india_confirm_34214.out`, PBS server log); all reported results come from the completed attempt 34223 (6 Oct 2026, 18:36–18:48 IST) | None: no result of the stopped attempts exists, and nothing in the method, data or analysis changed between attempts |
